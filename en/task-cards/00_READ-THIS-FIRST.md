## What you will find here

Each task card covers one job: adding a user, duplicating a campaign, setting up a tournament. This is not a manual you read front to back, but a set of cards you pick up the moment you are about to do something. Print them or keep them open next to your screen.

Every card has the same structure, so you always know where to look:

| Section | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| 📌 **Highlights** | Where in the Control Panel, when to use it, what you need, which role you need. Always read this first |
| 🚀 **Step by step** | The task itself, one step at a time |
| ⭐ **Golden rules & common mistakes** | What goes wrong in practice. Do not skip this — it is where the experience sits |
| ✅ **Checklist** | Tick list before you hit save or publish |
| 🔍 **Done? How to check** | How to test it yourself, usually from the player environment |

## Where to start

New to the Control Panel? Work through these four in this order. After that you can pick up any other card on its own.

1. **TK-01 — Add and manage users.** You get to know the Users screen, where most actions start.
2. **TK-05 — Manage tags and bulk assign.** Tags control who sees which campaign. Without tags, the rest only half works.
3. **TK-06 — Create and publish a campaign.** The backbone of every learning journey.
4. **TK-07 — Add and edit a challenge.** The chapters inside a campaign.

After that you have a campaign with a challenge, and you can add modules to it: TK-09 (micro game), TK-10 (quiz), TK-11 (survey), TK-12 (questionnaire) and TK-25 (text and video). Want to use an interactive video? Build it in HiHaHo first with TK-26.

## Three things you will need often

**Looking for a button and cannot find it? Check the three dots.** Many actions in the Control Panel sit behind the three dots to the right of a title: invite, export, bulk assign tags, achievements, If This Then That, duplicate and copy. This is the most common "it is not there" mistake.

**Tags decide who sees what.** A campaign filtered on tags is invisible to users without that tag. If something does not show up for a player, always check the tags first.

**Test as a player, not as a manager.** Almost every card ends with a check from the player environment: menu → **View app**. What you see as a manager is not what a player sees.

## What the markers mean

When you come across **[still to verify]** in a card, it means we have not yet confirmed that point in practice. At the bottom of that card you will find a **Still to verify** block: what exactly is open, how it will be checked, and who is doing it.

We would rather hand you something useful now with an honest note, than make you wait until every detail has been nailed down. Cards are updated as soon as the answers come in.

Two cards deserve extra attention:

- **TK-15 (leagues)** and **TK-19 (If This Then That)** describe fairly new functionality. Talk to 4Growth before you use them with a client or a large group.
- **TK-12 (questionnaire)** is the most complex module type. Build your first questionnaire together with us.

## Terminology: three to watch

The screens and the documentation sometimes use different words for the same thing:

| Dutch screens | English screens |
|---|---|
| **Campagne** | Campaign |
| **Label** | Tag |
| **Vaardigheid** | Skill |
| **Interactieve Video** | Gamified video |
| **Vervolgpaden** | If This Then That |

The English cards follow the English screen labels. Where a button name in a card does not match exactly what you see on screen, tell us — we would rather fix it than have you guessing.
