# 🎯 Task card TK-07 — Add & edit a challenge

> **For game managers.** How to add a challenge (chapter) to a campaign and set up its summary screen — no technical knowledge needed.
> **Status:** v1.0 · 30-08-2026 · part of the LearnStrike Knowledge Base · language: EN.

---

**A challenge is a "chapter" in your campaign.** Each challenge bundles a handful of modules around one theme or learning objective. When the player finishes, they see a summary screen: passed or failed, with your own text and image.

---

## 📌 Highlights

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Control Panel → **Campaigns** → [campaign] → **Add challenge** |
| **When to use** | Every new theme or learning objective within a campaign; rule of thumb: 3–5 modules per challenge |
| **What you need** | Name + description (per language), challenge image (required), texts and images for the summary screen |
| **Summary screen** | Separate text + image for **passed** and for **failed** |
| **Languages** | Multilingual organisation? Fill in the name, description and summary screen texts **for every language** |
| **Permissions** | Manager role (or higher), 2FA active |

---

## 🚀 Step by step

1. Open the **Control Panel** → **Campaigns** and click the campaign open.
2. Click **Add challenge**.
3. Fill in the basics:
   - **Name** (required) and **Description** — for each available language (tabs above the field).
   - **Image** (required) — select or upload (PNG/JPG, max 10 MB).
4. Set up the **summary screen**:
   - **Text for a passed challenge** + **Image for a passed challenge** — what the player sees on success.
   - **Text for a failed challenge** + **Image for a failed challenge** — what the player sees when it did not work out (yet).
   - Fill in these texts per language as well.
5. Click **Save**. The challenge appears in the campaign's challenge list.
6. **Want to change the order?** Drag and drop challenges in the list into the right position. Note: if the campaign has "Challenges must be played in order" switched on, this order also sets the playing route.
7. Next, add modules to the challenge (TK-09 through TK-12 and TK-25).
8. You can **edit** at any time: open the challenge → **Edit challenge**.
9. **Need to delete one?** Choose **Delete challenge**. Did it by accident? **Restore** brings it back, content and all.

---

## ⭐ Golden rules & common mistakes

- **Don't forget the failed screen.** Managers often only fill in the passed version; players who don't make it then land on a blank screen.
- **Write the failed screen to motivate.** "So close! Play again and improve your score" works better than "Sorry, you failed".
- **Multilingual? Fill in every language** — including the summary screen texts. An empty language shows empty texts.
- **Order = playing route** in campaigns that enforce the order. After dragging, check that chapter 1 really is at the top.
- **A challenge without modules is an empty chapter.** Only publish the campaign once every challenge has at least one module.
- **Deleted is not gone.** You can bring deleted challenges back with **Restore**.
- Extra options per challenge — **QR code** and **If This Then That** (conditional routing) — are outside the scope of this card; leave them alone to begin with.

---

## ✅ Checklist

- [ ] Name + description in every language your organisation uses
- [ ] Image uploaded (style consistent with the campaign)
- [ ] Passed summary screen: text + image
- [ ] Failed summary screen: text + image (worded to motivate)
- [ ] Challenge dragged into the right position in the order
- [ ] At least one module added

---

## 🔍 Done? How to check

Play the challenge through yourself in the player environment (menu → **View app**). Check the images and texts, and test **both** summary screens: pass the challenge once and deliberately fail it once. Is the order right compared to the other challenges?
