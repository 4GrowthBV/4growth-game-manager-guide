# 🔗 Task card TK-18 — Direct Play & public micro game

> **For game managers.** How to make a stand-alone Micro game playable: inside your own environment through a dashboard tile (Direct Play), or outside it through a shareable link (public micro game).
> **Status:** v1.0 · 30-08-2026 · part of the LearnStrike Knowledge Base · language: EN.

> ⚠️ **Heads-up:** this part of LearnStrike has changed since April 2026. This card is being updated. In doubt? Email support@4growth.com.

---

**Two features, two worlds — don't mix them up.**

**Direct Play** opens a Micro game straight away, without a Campaign or Challenge structure around it. So there is no intro screen and no closing screen; the player steps straight into the Unity module. Direct Play is meant for your **own logged-in players**, and you make it available through a **dashboard tile**.

**Public micro game** is the feature for anything you want to share **outside your environment**: social media, a newsletter, a recruitment campaign. This link works without a login.

⚠️ **Never share a Direct Play link externally.** If you want to share a game outside your environment, always use **Public micro games**. Direct Play exists for the dashboard of your own players.

---

## 📌 Highlights

| | Direct Play | Public micro game |
|---|---|---|
| **Where** | Control Panel → **Direct Play** | Control Panel → **Public micro games** |
| **Who it's for** | Your own users (logged in) | Anyone, no login needed |
| **How they reach it** | Through a **dashboard tile** in the player environment | Through a shareable link you copy |
| **Share externally** | **No** | Yes, that's what it's for |
| **What the player sees** | The Micro game straight away — no intro or closing screen | The Micro game straight away, without logging in |
| **Extras** | High score can be hidden, fullscreen mode | Open Graph description + image (both required) for a tidy preview in social and chat |
| **Scores** | Count towards the profile of the logged-in player | Anonymous scores are **not** stored |
| **Permissions** | Manager role (or higher), 2FA enabled | Manager role (or higher), 2FA enabled |

## 🚀 Step by step A — Direct Play (your own players, via a dashboard tile)

1. Open the **Control Panel** → **Direct Play** → **Add**.
2. Fill in: **Name** and **Description** (per language) and an **Image**.
3. Choose the **Micro game** (required).
4. Set the options:
   - **Hide high score** — tick this if players shouldn't see each other's top scores (for practice or assessment games, for instance).
   - **Enable fullscreen mode** — play the Micro game full screen (recommended on mobile).
5. Tick **Add dashboard tile**. This is how players find the game — without a tile, a Direct Play is effectively impossible to find. More about laying out the dashboard: TK-21.
6. Click **Save**.
7. Check in the player environment (menu → **View app**) that the tile is there and the game plays.

## 🚀 Step by step B — Public micro game (sharing externally)

1. Open the **Control Panel** → **Public micro games** → **Add**.
2. Fill in the **Name** (one language, not multilingual).
3. Choose the **Micro game** (required).
4. Fill in the **Open Graph description** and **Open Graph image** (both required). This is what people see when the link is shared on LinkedIn, WhatsApp or Teams: an image plus text as a preview card. So make it appealing.
5. Click **Save**. You return to the **Public micro games** overview.
6. **Copying the link:** on the row for your game — the one with the name and the identifier — there are **four icons** on the right. The **second icon from the left** is the copy icon. Click it and the URL is on your computer's clipboard.
7. Paste the link wherever you need it (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V): in a message, a newsletter or a post. Players don't have to log in.

## ⭐ Golden rules & common mistakes

- **Sharing externally? Always public micro games.** Direct Play links do not belong outside your own environment. Use a Direct Play link externally and a player without an account gets stuck.
- **A Direct Play without a dashboard tile is unfindable.** Always tick the tile.
- **No intro or closing screen with Direct Play.** Do you want to give the player context beforehand or reflection afterwards? Then you need a Challenge with modules around it (TK-07, TK-25), not a Direct Play.
- **Think about what you make public.** Does the Micro game contain company-specific or sensitive content? Then don't publish it as a public micro game — that link is accessible to everyone and can be forwarded.
- **Anonymous scores disappear.** Anyone playing through a public link builds up no progress and no score. If you want to measure results, have players log in and work with a Campaign.
- **The Open Graph image is your calling card.** Use a landscape image with little text, and test the preview by pasting the link into a chat.
- **A public micro game as a recruitment tool:** combine it with a Campaign announcement — let people try it, then point them to registration.

## ✅ Checklist before you share

- [ ] Right feature chosen: a dashboard tile for your own players (Direct Play) or a shareable link for the outside world (public micro game)
- [ ] Micro game played through yourself and checked for content
- [ ] Direct Play: high score setting arranged and dashboard tile ticked
- [ ] Public micro game: Open Graph text and image filled in and preview tested
- [ ] Link tested yourself on desktop **and** mobile

---

## 🔍 Done? How to check

**Direct Play:** open the player environment (menu → **View app**) and check that the tile is there and the game plays. **Public micro game:** open the copied link in a private/incognito window (so without logging in) — does the game play straight away? Then paste the link into a chat message and check that the image and description show up neatly as a preview.
