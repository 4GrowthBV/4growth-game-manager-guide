# 🎯 Task card TK-23 — Insights & exports

> **For game managers.** How to follow players' progress and results, and get all your data out of the platform as Excel files.
> **Status:** v1.0 · 30-08-2026 · part of the LearnStrike Knowledge Base · language: EN.

> ⚠️ **Heads-up:** this part of LearnStrike has changed since April 2026. This card is being updated. In doubt? Email support@4growth.com.

---

**Insights is your measuring station.** You click from campaign to challenge to module and see at every level who played what and who scored what. For reporting you export to Excel — from a single progress overview to a full export with everything in it.

---

## 📌 Highlights

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where** | Control Panel → **Insights** |
| **Navigation** | Per campaign → per challenge → per module; plus insights per user |
| **Exports** | Progress per campaign (XLSX) · survey answers (XLSX) · full export (ZIP with 17 Excel sheets) · leaderboard standings (XLSX, see TK-20) |
| **Full export** | Choose a date range → it is built in the background → **the download link arrives by email** |
| **When to use** | Tracking progress during a campaign, evaluating afterwards, reporting to a client |
| **Permissions** | Manager role (or higher), 2FA enabled |

⚠️ **Can't find the email with the download link, or is there no link in it?** Some email programs block links in messages like this. It happens in the locally installed Outlook app, for example, because of a security setting. Open the same email in the web version of your email program — the link works there.

---

## 🚀 Step by step

**A. Viewing progress**

1. Open the **Control Panel** → **Insights**. You'll see the overview of your campaigns.
2. Click a **campaign** for the detail view, with a **progress screen**: who has started, who has finished. There's an **Export** button here (XLSX).
3. Click through to a **challenge** and then a **module** for detailed figures per part.
4. For one specific player: go to **Users → [user] → insights** — that person's progress and survey answers.

**B. Exporting survey results**

5. In Insights, open the survey module in question. You can create two exports:
   - **Aggregated** — totals per question, for quick analysis.
   - **Individual answers** — every answer per player, for qualitative analysis.
6. Watch the **"only latest"** option: with it, only each player's most recent answer counts — handy if players were allowed to fill in the survey more than once.

**C. Creating a full export**

7. In Insights, go to **Export** (the export form) and choose a **date range**.
8. Start the export. It is built **in the background** — with large data sets this takes a while. You don't have to sit and wait on the screen.
9. As soon as the export is ready you'll receive an **email with the download link**. If you can't see that link, open the email in the web version of your email program (see the warning above).
10. The result is a **ZIP with 17 Excel sheets**, including: GameProgress (campaign progress), ModuleSessions (play sessions per module), QuizAnswers, SurveyAnswers, QuestionnaireAnswers, Certificates, Achievements, Users, Challenges and Modules.

## 💡 What to use when

| Goal | Use this |
|---|---|
| Tracking progress while the campaign runs | Progress screen per campaign (+ export as a snapshot) |
| Helping or nudging an individual player | Insights per user |
| Evaluating reflection or survey outcomes | Survey exports (aggregated for the overview, individual for the depth) |
| Final report to a client | Full export over the campaign period + leaderboard export (TK-20) |
| Judging the effect of a single module | Module detail in Insights (ModuleSessions from the full export, for instance) |

---

## ⭐ Golden rules & common mistakes

- **Choose the date range deliberately.** Too wide a range makes the full export slow and pollutes your report with old data.
- **Keep an eye on your mailbox after a full export.** The link arrives by email, so don't start the export just before you go offline.
- **Export at fixed moments.** The figures change constantly; agree a reference date with your client and export then.
- **Privacy first.** Exports contain personal data (names, email addresses, answers). Share aggregated figures wherever you can; share individual data only with people who really need it.
- **Choose "only latest" deliberately** for surveys — otherwise you count repeat submissions twice.

---

## ✅ Checklist for a report

- [ ] Reference date/date range agreed with the client
- [ ] Progress export per campaign created
- [ ] Survey exports created (with a deliberate choice on "only latest")
- [ ] Full export requested and the email with the download link received
- [ ] ZIP checked (do the sheets open?)
- [ ] Personal data filtered out where it isn't needed

---

## 🔍 Done? How to check

Open the exported files in Excel: do the numbers match what you see in the Insights screen? Spot check: look up one test account in GameProgress and compare it with the progress on screen.
